How to

Drag any token to move it. Tap a token for its actions.
Double-tap a token to type a 2–3 letter label (MES, GK, CB…).
Link tool: tap two tokens to connect them. Arrow/Run: drag on the grass.
Zone: drag a box, double-tap it to rename (e.g. 3v3). Erase: tap anything to delete it.
Swipe left/right on empty grass to change board. Everything saves automatically.
